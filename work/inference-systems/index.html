RESEARCH & ENGINEERING

Overview

Running a language model involves a series of practical tradeoffs. Responsiveness, resource use, and reliability all matter to the experience of using it.

I am interested in the fundamentals of inference performance and in how to reason about bottlenecks using reproducible, publicly discussable experiments.

Topics I explore

  1. Latency and throughput as engineering concepts
  2. Measuring responsiveness under changing workloads
  3. Resource efficiency and the user experience
  4. Clear measurement, repeatability, and communication
Good engineering starts with a useful question and a measurement you can trust.
