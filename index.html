The details

What I pay attention to.

Questions I return to when studying and building AI software.

How do we know it works?

Look beyond headline scores, test realistic tasks, and learn from examples where models make mistakes.

What makes it responsive?

Consider how performance, quality, and resource use shape the user experience.

What happens when things go wrong?

Design clear boundaries, give people control, and make failures easier to understand and recover from.
